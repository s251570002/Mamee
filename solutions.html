04 / SOLUTIONS

Creativity that became
real business action.

The solutions below are linked directly to documented MAMEE actions: product repositioning, market and partnership strategy, healthier-product development and technology/supply-chain investment.

01
PRODUCT INNOVATION

Turn instant noodles into a crunchy snack.

MAMEE used an observed consumer behaviour to create a different product format. The noodles were seasoned and repositioned as a snack, leading to Mamee Monster in 1974.

RESULTING IDEA
Mamee Monster noodle snack

02
MARKET + PARTNERSHIP INNOVATION

Work with local partners in international markets.

MIDA reports that MAMEE shifted from relying mainly on trade shows to finding local partners with established retailer networks in Europe and the US. The partners handled front-end sales while MAMEE managed manufacturing.

STRATEGIC BENEFIT
Better understanding of local demand + market access
MIDA evidence ↗
03
HEALTHIER PRODUCTS

Move into better-for-you and premium segments.

MAMEE responded to evolving consumer preferences by investing in healthier-product opportunities, including a 2020 partnership with US-based The Good Crisp Company and investment in The Golden Duck.

STRATEGIC BENEFIT
Access to niche and premium consumer needs
MIDA evidence ↗
04
TECHNOLOGY + OPERATIONS

Use automation, sourcing and localised production.

MIDA reports technological integration, diversified sourcing and localised production as pillars for managing supply-chain complexity. In 2024, MAMEE also announced up to RM150 million for a new production facility using the latest generation of automatic production technology.

INVESTMENT
RM150 million announced for production technology
MIDA investment report ↗
CREATIVITY IN ACTION

The solution was not always “make a new product.”

MAMEE's case shows several types of innovation: changing a product format, changing the route to market, entering better-for-you segments, building partnerships and investing in production technology.
